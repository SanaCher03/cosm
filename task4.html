About Us

Welcome to Sakura Hotel, a serene oasis in the heart of Tokyo where traditional Japanese hospitality meets modern luxury. Established in 1997, our hotel has been a sanctuary for travelers seeking an authentic cultural experience combined with world-class amenities.

Our architecture reflects the elegance of Japanese design, featuring serene Zen gardens, tatami mat rooms, and panoramic views of the Tokyo skyline. Each of our 120 rooms is meticulously designed to provide comfort while honoring Japanese aesthetics.

At Sakura Hotel, we pride ourselves on our "omotenashi" (Japanese hospitality) philosophy, ensuring every guest feels the warmth and attention to detail that defines Japanese service excellence.
